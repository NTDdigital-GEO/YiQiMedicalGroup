Our Team

Five doctors. One system.

YiQi means "coming together." Our doctors share one belief: that you can treat what is blocked and also strengthen the flow that keeps you well.

The story of YiQi

A group of TCM practitioners often met in a community park to talk about their patients: burned-out young professionals, older adults low on energy, athletes, new mothers. They had good tools, but something felt missing.

Then Tai-Chi master Sifu Donna joined them and said: "You treat the blockage, but what about the flow of the river itself?" That became YiQi: TCM doctors, dietitians and Tai-Chi masters working as one team.
