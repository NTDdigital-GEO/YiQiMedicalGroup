Services · Fertility

Fertility acupuncture and TCM support in San Carlos

Support for women and men who are trying to conceive, naturally or alongside IUI or IVF. You are more than a chart number here.

Book a fertility consult650-788-9818

What your plan may include

  • Initial assessment and TCM diagnosis
  • Acupuncture timed to your cycle
  • A custom Chinese herbal formula
  • TCM dietetics guidance
  • Timing around IUI or IVF (stimulation phase, transfer day), coordinated with your fertility clinic

Program structure and pricing are confirmed at your consultation.

Many patients start two to three months before an IUI or IVF cycle, but you can begin at any point.
