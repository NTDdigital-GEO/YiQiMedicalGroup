FAQ

Questions we hear most

How much does a visit cost?
Community clinic: $60 per visit (acupuncture only). Private acupuncture: $150 per session. Tui Na: $120, or 5 sessions for $500. Fertility and Acu-Facial pricing is confirmed at booking.
Do you accept insurance?
We accept PPO plans. Coverage for acupuncture varies by plan, so we verify your benefits before your first visit and tell you your expected cost.
Who can come to the $60 community clinic?
Mondays 3:00–6:00 pm: everyone. Thursdays 10:00 am–6:00 pm: seniors 65+. Last appointment 5:30 pm. Acupuncture only; pay per visit.
Does acupuncture hurt?
Most people feel a brief pinch or tingling at insertion, then little or nothing. The needles are about as thin as a hair, and many patients fall asleep.
What happens at my first visit?
Your doctor takes your history, checks your pulse and tongue, explains the reasoning and plan, and starts treatment in the same visit.
How many visits will I need?
It depends on your concern and how long you have had it. Many plans start with one to two visits per week; your doctor will give you a plan at the first visit.
Can acupuncture be combined with IUI or IVF?
Many patients combine them. We time acupuncture around your cycle and treatment dates, and we recommend that you tell your fertility clinic about your plan.
What is the difference between acupuncture and Tui Na?
Acupuncture uses thin needles at specific points. Tui Na is hands-on massage along the meridians, with acupressure and hot stone, and uses no needles.
I don't like needles. Are there other options?
Yes. Tui Na uses no needles. Tell us when you book and we will suggest the best fit.
How do I book or reschedule?
Book online at yiqi.janeapp.com, or call 650-788-9818.
Do you see patients in Chinese?
Our website is available in English and Chinese, and Dr. Ronkos is proficient in Mandarin.
