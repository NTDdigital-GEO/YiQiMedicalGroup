Common questions

Do you accept insurance?
We accept PPO plans. Acupuncture coverage varies by plan, so our front desk verifies your benefits before your first visit. The $60 community clinic is pay-per-visit and needs no insurance.
How much does a visit cost?
Community clinic: $60. Private acupuncture: $150 per session. Tui Na: $120, or 5 sessions for $500.
Does acupuncture hurt?
Most people feel a slight pinch or tingling for a moment. The needles are about as thin as a hair, and many patients relax enough to fall asleep.
What happens at my first visit?
Your doctor reviews your history, checks your pulse and tongue, explains the plan, and starts treatment in the same visit.
