中醫食療

子午流注：中醫養生時鐘

氣血在經絡中依一天的節律運行。讓飲食、作息與活動配合這個節律，有助於維持健康。

  • 子（23:00–01:00）是午夜時分，陰氣最盛。
  • 午（11:00–13:00）是正午時分，陽氣最盛。
  • 流注是指氣血在經絡中的流動與灌注。

此說以「天人合一」為基礎：人體隨自然節律運行。以下為衛教介紹，如何依您的狀況調整，請詢問醫師。

以下對照表保留英文原文，並附中文經絡名稱。

十二時辰

時間時辰與經絡建議飲食建議活動
23:00–01:00Zi 子時
Gallbladder 膽經		None (fasting/sleeping)Deep sleep; avoid screens, eating, and strenuous activity; keep the room dark and quiet
01:00–03:00Chou 丑時
Liver 肝經		—Continue deep sleep; avoid alcohol, late-night eating, and emotional stress; the liver does its detoxifying work during sleep
03:00–05:00Yin 寅時
Lung 肺經		—Sleep; if awake, practice slow deep breathing; avoid cold drinks and exposure to cold air
05:00–07:00Mao 卯時
Large Intestine 大腸經		Warm water on an empty stomach; light fiber-rich foods (e.g., oatmeal, prunes)Drink warm water, bowel movement, light stretching or walking; avoid a heavy breakfast at this time
07:00–09:00Chen 辰時
Stomach 胃經		Warm, easily digestible breakfast: congee, eggs, steamed vegetables, whole grains; avoid cold or raw foodsEat breakfast slowly; do not skip it; a light walk after eating aids digestion
09:00–11:00Si 巳時
Spleen 脾經		If snacking: warm cooked foods, ginger tea, small amounts of fruit; avoid cold drinks and raw foodsBest time for mental work, studying or decision-making; avoid overeating or heavy physical strain
11:00–13:00Wu 午時
Heart 心經		Balanced lunch: lean protein, vegetables, whole grains; moderate portions; avoid excessive caffeine or alcoholEat lunch, then rest or take a short nap (15–30 minutes); avoid intense exercise or emotional stress
13:00–15:00Wei 未時
Small Intestine 小腸經		Warm water or herbal tea; easily digestible snacks if needed; avoid heavy or greasy foodsLight activity or walking; allow digestion; avoid vigorous exercise right after lunch
15:00–17:00Shen 申時
Bladder 膀胱經		Warm water or herbal tea; hydrating foods (e.g., cucumber, watermelon in moderation); avoid excessive saltDrink water, stretch, take a walk; a good time for physical work or exercise; do not hold urine
17:00–19:00You 酉時
Kidney 腎經		Light, warm dinner: soups, steamed fish, leafy greens, black beans, walnuts; avoid heavy, salty or spicy foodsEat dinner early and in moderation; gentle walking; avoid strenuous exercise; start winding down
19:00–21:00Xu 戌時
Pericardium 心包經		Herbal tea (e.g., chamomile, lavender); light snacks if needed; avoid caffeine and heavy mealsRelax: gentle yoga, meditation, reading, time with loved ones; avoid work and screens
21:00–23:00Hai 亥時
Triple Burner 三焦經		None, or a small sip of warm water; avoid eating and alcoholPrepare for sleep: dim lights, avoid screens, take a warm bath, relax or meditate; aim to be asleep by 23:00
