What is acupuncture?什麼是針灸？

Acupuncture places thin, sterile needles at specific points on the body. Traditional Chinese Medicine describes the pathways between these points as meridians, and the energy that moves through them as qi. In plain terms: the goal is to help your body circulate, calm and repair itself.針灸是將細小的無菌針，放在身體特定的穴位上。中醫把穴位之間的通路稱為經絡，在其中流動的能量稱為「氣」。簡單說，目標是幫助身體循環、平靜並自我修復。

Is it painful?會痛嗎？

You may feel a slight pinch or tingling as a needle goes in; it fades almost at once. Once the needles are in place, most people feel little or nothing, and many fall asleep. The needles are about as thin as a hair.進針時可能有輕微刺感或酥麻，很快就消失。針留置後，多數人幾乎沒有感覺，不少人會睡著。針細如髮絲。

TCM Dietetics中醫食療

Eat well. Live well. Age well.吃得好，活得好，老得好。

In Traditional Chinese Medicine, food is part of treatment. The aim is to eat for your body's current state and the season, not only for calories.在中醫裡，飲食是治療的一部分。目標是依您目前的身體狀態與季節來吃，而不只是計算熱量。

1

WHAT to eat吃什麼

Foods run warming or cooling. Cold and tired: favor warming foods such as ginger or lamb. Hot and stressed: favor cooling foods such as cucumber or mung beans. Heavy and bloated: favor drying foods such as corn or celery.食物有溫涼之分。怕冷疲倦：多吃薑、羊肉等溫性食物；燥熱緊繃：多吃小黃瓜、綠豆等涼性食物；沉重脹氣：多吃玉米、芹菜等利濕食物。

2

WHEN to eat何時吃

Digestion is strongest around midday. Make lunch (11 am–1 pm) your largest meal and keep dinner (5–7 pm) lighter.中午前後消化力最強。午餐（11 點至 1 點）吃最多，晚餐（5 點至 7 點）吃得較清淡。

3

HOW to eat怎麼吃

Prefer warm, cooked meals and drink warm water or tea with food. Cold drinks and ice cream weaken digestion.以溫熱熟食為主，用餐時喝溫水或茶。冰飲與冰淇淋會削弱消化。
